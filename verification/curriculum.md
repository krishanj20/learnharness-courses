## Scope

Review uses only the skill, section, physical page and source citation attached to each saved objective. Section counts show the breadth of the generated plan. The substantive check is whether a learner can demonstrate the source skills in the table. A section assignment or a matching citation alone cannot establish that coverage.

## Observed coverage

At 11:07 UTC, Chapter 1 had 53 objectives across all five sections. All 53 citations matched the indicated physical page using an independent PDF text extractor after normalising typography and spacing. The objectives covered the contract and venue comparisons, both sides of the market, several mechanisms for excess return and the stages of the investment process. The plan is demanding for a single chapter. The market-participants section alone has 19 objectives, some of which combine several skills. The thirteen other chapters had no saved objectives at this reading.
