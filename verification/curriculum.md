## Scope

The chapter expectations above were set from the source outline, body and opening chapter questions before the first generated plan. Review uses only each saved objective’s skill, section, physical page, source citation and supporting topics. It compares the outcomes with the chapter expectations and checks the underlying concepts against the section and subsection headings.

## Observed coverage

All 101 citations matched the cited text in the correct section on the designated physical page. An independent pypdf comparison found either the full citation or a distinctive phrase for 99 of the 101 objectives. The two equation-heavy citations needed direct inspection of the worked procedure on physical p. 279 and the tracking-error derivation on p. 404. Both led to the intended material.

Each named subheading within a teaching section appeared in its associated outcome or supporting topics. Reading those outcomes against the chapter expectations found no substantial topic omission. For example, Chapter 8 covers the effect of sampling noise as well as PCA estimation and interpretation. Chapter 10 distinguishes errors in the return forecast from errors in risk estimation. Chapter 12 separates a tradable hedge from a portfolio that mimics a non-tradable series. Chapter 14 distinguishes attribution error, selection, and sizing.

Chapter 3’s saved outcome now states the Gaussian assumption behind the simplified Sharpe-ratio standard error. Its supporting note explains that the general independent-return calculation also depends on skewness and kurtosis. The source citation still points to the original wording on physical p. 107. Both the corrected outcome and the retained citation were verified in the saved course. [Liu, Rekkas and Wong, equations 2.13–2.14](https://doi.org/10.1155/2012/878561)
